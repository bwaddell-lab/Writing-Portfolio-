Frequently Asked Questions

Customer Support Documentation Sample

Account Access

How do I reset my password?

Select the “Forgot Password” option on the login page. Enter your email address and follow the password reset instructions sent to your inbox.

Why am I locked out of my account?

Accounts may become temporarily locked after multiple failed login attempts. Wait 15 minutes before attempting to log in again.

How do I contact support?

Users can submit a support ticket through the Help Center portal or contact technical support by phone during business hours.

Uploading Documents

What file types can I upload?

Supported file types include PDF, JPG, PNG, and DOCX.

Why is my document upload failing?

Ensure the file size is under the maximum upload limit and verify that your internet connection is stable.
