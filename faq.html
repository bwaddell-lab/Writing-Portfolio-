Starting and Managing Your Tax Return

Below you'll find a guide with answers to the most frequently asked questions for online tax preparation.

How do I start a new tax return?

After signing into your account, select the “Start New Return” option from the dashboard. The platform will guide you through each section of the filing process, including personal information, income details, deductions, and credits.

How do I review my return before submitting?

Select the “Review Return” option before filing. The system will scan your return for missing information, calculation errors, and incomplete sections that may require additional attention.

What happens after I file?

Once your return is submitted, you will receive a confirmation notification indicating that your filing was successfully transmitted. Filing status updates will appear in your account dashboard as your return is processed.

Refund and Filing Status

How do I track my refund?

Refund status information is available from the “Refund Tracker” section within your account dashboard. Additional updates may also be available through the IRS “Where’s My Refund?” tool.

Can I save my progress and return later?

Yes. Your information is automatically saved as you complete your return. You may securely sign out at any time and continue where you left off during your next login session.

Why is my refund delayed?

Refund processing times may vary depending on filing volume, verification requirements, or incomplete information included in the return. Continue monitoring your account for updated status information.

What does “Pending” mean?

A “Pending” status means your return has been received and is currently being processed. Additional review may be required before the return is fully accepted.

How do I check filing status?

Filing status information is available from the “My Returns” section of your account dashboard. Status updates may include Pending, Accepted, Rejected, or Completed.

How do I contact support?

Customers contact technical support by phone during business hours, or by submitting a work ticket via the portal on your account.

Uploading Documents

What file types can I upload?

Supported file types include PDF, JPG, PNG, and DOCX.

Why is my document upload failing?

Ensure the file size is under the maximum upload limit and verify that your internet connection is stable.
