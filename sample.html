Beginner Investing Guide

What is investing?

Investing can feel confusing, but it does not have to be.

Investing means growing your money over time. Instead of letting your money sit, you can put it into assets like stocks, which are small pieces of companies that may increase in value.

Here are three simple ways to get started:

  • Start small: You do not need a lot of money to begin.
  • Start early: Time can help your money grow.
  • Diversify: Spread your money across different investments instead of putting it all in one place.
Investing is not about knowing everything on day one. It is about starting with a plan, learning as you go, and staying consistent over time.
