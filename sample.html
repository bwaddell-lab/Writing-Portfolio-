How to Submit an IT Support Ticket

User Guide Sample | Technical Writing Portfolio

Technical issues are sometimes unavoidable. When they happen, employees should know how to report the issue clearly so the support team can respond quickly.

Purpose

This guide explains how to submit and track an IT help ticket through the internal support portal.

Before You Begin

  • Make sure you are logged into your computer.
  • Confirm that you have access to the company home page or employee portal.
  • Gather any screenshots, error messages, or documents that may help explain the issue.

Steps to Submit a Ticket

  1. Go to the employee home page. Open the company home page or internal support portal from your computer.
  2. Find the support menu. On the left side of the screen, look for the task bar with the list of navigation buttons.
  3. Select the ticket option. Click the second-to-last button in the task bar. This will open the IT support ticket page.
  4. Describe the issue. In the ticket form, explain what problem you are experiencing. Be specific about what happened, when it happened, and what you were trying to do.
  5. Add supporting materials. Upload screenshots, documents, or error messages that may help the support team understand the problem.
  6. Submit the ticket. Review your information for accuracy, then click the submit button.

After You Submit

After the ticket is submitted, you should receive a confirmation number. Use this number to track the status of your request.

Tips for a Strong Ticket

  • Use clear and specific language.
  • Include the name of the application or device involved.
  • Attach screenshots when possible.
  • Avoid vague descriptions such as “it does not work.”
